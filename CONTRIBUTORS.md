# Software contributors and attribution

The laboratory has confirmed that the following three contributors agree in principle to open-source publication of their software contributions (confirmation supplied by Maja Pakula Lundheim on 22 October 2026):

- **Nicolas Taulier** — initial development of the acoustic acquisition application.
- **Tianhao Dong** — subsequent software improvements and acoustic analysis scripts. 
- **Maja Pakula Lundheim** — subsequent software improvements, experimental workflow, integration and manuscript-related documentation.


The three contributors and laboratory have been reported by the project contact to authorize distribution of their contributions under the **MIT License** (22 October 2026); see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). The proposed citation order is Nicolas Taulier, Tianhao Dong, Maja Pakula Lundheim; **the precise citation order remains to be approved** before publication. The MIT grant does not cover third-party packages, vendor SDKs, or unlicensed images.

The new `analysis/adv_analysis.py` CLI is a refactoring and compatibility implementation prepared from the uploaded procedures. Its numerical software tests do not constitute experimental validation, and it has not yet been reviewed on the real instrument or against a representative recording.
